1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & ARCHITECTURE OVERVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The "We Can Change" (WCC) enterprise system consists of a RESTful Express.js backend 
(`wcc_api`) connected to MongoDB Atlas and a high-performance Next.js 16 (Turbopack) 
frontend web application (`wcc_web`).

The system implements a strict Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) model supporting:
  - Admin (Full operational, member verification, financial ledger, and audit control)
  - Member (Official digital membership ID, directory, and expense claim reimbursement)
  - Volunteer (Digital volunteer badge, live service hour logging, and community drives)
  - Public Guest (Public landing site, focus areas, leadership profile, and ID verification)
