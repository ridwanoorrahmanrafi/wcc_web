1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & ARCHITECTURE OVERVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The "We Can Change" (WCC) platform consists of a RESTful Express.js 5.2.1 backend
(`wcc_api`) and a Next.js 16.3.5 (Turbopack) frontend web application (`wcc_web`).

The system implements a strict Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) model supporting:
  - Admin: Full operational oversight, member approvals, request reviews, financial
    management, and system audit trail inspection.
  - Finance Officer: Financial transaction ledger, accounts management, advance
    settlements, and reimbursement approval workflows.
  - Member: Official digital membership ID card with dynamic QR verification,
    member directory access, wing change requests, and expense reimbursement claims.
  - Volunteer: Volunteer Corps portal, digital service badge, service hour logging,
    history log review, wing transfer requests, and upcoming community drives.
  - Public Guest: High-converting landing site, focus areas, leadership message,
    and public QR member verification.

     bcrypt hashed passwords, totalHours counter.
  2. Member: Central member registry, bilingual names (Bangla & English), blood groups,
     upazilas, wings, statuses, photo URLs, joined dates.
  3. MemberRequest: Wing transfer and volunteer upgrade requests with administrative
     approval/rejection workflows and notes.
  4. Account: Bank, Mobile Banking (MFS), and Cash liquidity accounts with real-time balances.
  5. Activity: Community campaigns, welfare drives, budgets, itemized expenses, and variances.
  6. Transaction: Master double-entry ledger tracking all institutional financial movements.
  7. Income: Inflow records from grants, donations, and membership dues.
  8. Expense: Outflow expenditure records categorized by operational and program tags.
  9. Reimbursement: Personal expense claims submitted by members/volunteers with review states.
  10. Advance: Petty cash disbursements, activity advances, and receipt settlements.
  11. Vendor: External service providers, contractors, and supplier registry.
  12. VolunteerLog: Activity-based volunteer service hours, drive names, and timestamps.
  13. AuditLog: Automated immutable audit trail of state changes, financial actions, and reviews.
